### About the App

This application visualizes the computation of the PEM Severity Score (PSS), a metric that
quantifies the severity of post-exertional malaise (PEM). The app allows the user to rate
five symptoms by their respective frequency and severity and watch the score
be derived step by step.

### Workflow

The five symptom questions are taken from the DSQ-PEM (Cotler et al., 2018), each rated on
two 0-4 scales. The PSS is the average of the per-question harmonic means of
frequency and severity, normalized to a 0-1 range. Based on the result, a severity state
is derived. Computation follows these four steps:  
*1. Feasibility:* At least three of five questions must be fully answered.  
*2. Necessity:* Based on the evaluation guidelines of the DSQ-PEM, PEM is present only
if at least one question has frequency &ge; 2 and severity &ge; 2. Otherwise the PSS is set to 0.  
*3. Calculation:* The answered questions are combined into the score.  
*4. Categorization:* The score is mapped onto four severity states: not affected (=0),
mildly affected (&lt; 0.5), moderately affected (&lt; 0.75) and severely affected (&le; 1.0).
A gauge visualizes this aspect.
