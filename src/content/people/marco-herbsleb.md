Marco Herbsleb is a sport scientist and postdoctoral researcher in clinical exercise physiology at the Exercise Immunology and Health Lab, Friedrich Schiller University Jena. He also works as a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Psychosomatic Medicine and Psychotherapy, Jena University Hospital.
His research focuses on cardiorespiratory fitness, autonomic cardiovascular regulation, and physiological responses to exercise in healthy individuals and clinical populations.
Using standardised exercise testing, heart rate and heart rate variability analysis, and functional performance assessments, he investigates exercise capacity and individual responses to physical exercise.
A particular focus lies on the mechanisms underlying reduced exercise capacity and exercise intolerance in people with psychiatric disorders, as well as on the potential of individually tailored exercise interventions. His more recent research explores the relationships between physical activity, cardiorespiratory fitness, brain health, cognition, and neuroplasticity in older adults and endurance-trained individuals. The overarching aim of his work is to translate physiological insights into evidence-based assessment and exercise interventions.
