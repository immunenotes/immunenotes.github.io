Sport scientist who has been working in the documentation, management, and analysis of behavioral, medical, and sports science data since 2009. Since 2011, he has also been actively involved in university teaching. He earned his Ph.D. (Dr. phil.) at Friedrich Schiller University Jena with a research focus on heart rate variability analysis. His research interests include heart rate variability, heart rate monitoring, recovery and regeneration, as well as cardiopulmonary exercise and physiological responses to physical stress.
