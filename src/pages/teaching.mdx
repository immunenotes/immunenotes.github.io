# Teaching

Within the B.Sc. programme in Sport Science — Performance & Health, Prof. Puta holds module
responsibility for six core modules spanning scientific foundations, sports medicine, and
health promotion (AMS I, GSA, GSM, VSM I, VSM II, NSW); at the M.Sc. level, he heads the two
Sports Medicine modules covering exercise physiology, immunology, and sports medical
diagnostics and data analysis (M-SMED1, M-SMED2).

## Immunenotes apps

Immunenotes apps are provided for teaching and research purposes exclusively. The use of the
apps is restricted to lectures, seminars and projects developed by Christian Puta and Marc Gabriel.

Visit the Immunenotes app server <a href="https://spomed.svwiswp05.uni-jena.de/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><u>here</u></a>.
